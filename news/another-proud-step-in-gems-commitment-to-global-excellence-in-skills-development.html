Another Proud Step in GEMS’ Commitment to Global Excellence in Skills Development

September 1, 2025

GEMS has once again taken a remarkable step forward in its journey of excellence. The GEMS City & Guilds Accreditation Ceremony was recently held at Denning, where prestigious guests, including Ms. Gulmina Bilal, Chairperson of the National Vocational and Technical Training Commission (NAVTTC), and Dr. Ghulam Ali Mallah, Executive Director of the Inter Boards Coordination Commission (IBCC), graced the occasion. Their presence highlighted the importance of skill-based education in Pakistan’s development journey. The ceremony celebrated a landmark achievement: the awarding of the City & Guilds Accreditation Certificate to Denning by City & Guilds, UK a Royal Chartered institution recognized globally for its world-class training and certification standards.

Why City & Guilds Accreditation Matters

Receiving accreditation from City & Guilds is more than an institutional milestone; it is a transformation in opportunity. City & Guilds certifications are recognized and trusted worldwide, offering learners the chance to:

  • Obtain internationally recognized qualifications.
  • Secure global job opportunities in competitive markets.
  • Contribute confidently to the global workforce with future-ready skills.

This recognition places Denning at the forefront of vocational and professional education in Pakistan, ensuring students not only gain knowledge but also earn qualifications that are respected by employers across the world.

Courses Offered at Denning under City & Guilds Accreditation

Denning, now an accredited centre, provides learners with access to high-end courses that prepare them for career success at both national and international levels. Some of the top programs include:

  • Business and Management Training
  • Information Technology and Digital Skills
  • Technical and Vocational Programs
  • Hospitality and Culinary Arts
  • Health and Social Care

Each of these courses is designed to meet global industry standards, combining theoretical learning with practical skills. Learners at Denning benefit from highly qualified faculty, industry-driven curriculum, and mentorship from experienced professionals.

Driving Excellence Together

The success of this accreditation is the result of visionary leadership and dedicated teamwork. The ceremony acknowledged the contributions of:

  • Tabish Fareedi, Country Manager of GEMS
  • Muhammad Furqan Iqbal, Academic Head
  • Muhammad Irshad, Quality Assurance Head
  • Ms. Gulmina Bilal, Chairperson NAVTTC

Together, they are committed to shaping a skilled, confident, and future-ready Pakistan.

The Importance of Accredited Courses in Today’s World

In today’s rapidly evolving job market, accredited courses make all the difference. Employers across the globe prefer candidates with certifications that reflect quality training and recognized standards. With City & Guilds-accredited programs, students at Denning gain:

  • Enhanced employability and career growth prospects.
  • Skills aligned with international market demands.
  • Pathways to work, study, or migrate abroad with confidence.
  • Assurance of quality education backed by globally respected institutions.

By enrolling in these accredited courses, learners invest in a future where their qualifications hold value not only in Pakistan but across the world.

Paving the Way Toward a Skilled Pakistan

At GEMS and Denning, the vision is clear: to empower Pakistan’s youth with the skills, knowledge, and confidence to compete on the world stage. This partnership with City & Guilds is a milestone that reflects the commitment to quality, excellence, and global recognition.

For more details on programs and enrollment, visit Denning and explore how you can become part of this journey toward a brighter, future-ready Pakistan.
