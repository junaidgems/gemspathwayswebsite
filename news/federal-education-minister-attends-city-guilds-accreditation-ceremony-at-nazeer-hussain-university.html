Federal Education Minister Attends City & Guilds Accreditation Ceremony at Nazeer Hussain University

June 20, 2025

Nazeer Hussain University (NHU) hosted its City & Guilds accreditation ceremony in Karachi, marking a significant step toward aligning local vocational training with international quality standards. The ceremony was graced by the Federal Minister for Education, Dr. Khalid Maqbool Siddiqui, who attended as chief guest, joined by senior officials from academia, global accreditation bodies, and international organizations.

The event commenced with the recitation of the Holy Qur’an and the National Anthem, followed by a warm welcome from NHU’s leadership. In his address, Engr. Farhat M. Khan, Chancellor of NHU, stated, “We are privileged to host Dr. Khalid Maqbool Siddiqui on this historic occasion. His presence reinforces our shared mission of delivering global-standard education to the youth of Pakistan.”

City & Guilds Accreditation – A Gateway to Global Skills Recognition

City & Guilds, a globally renowned UK-based vocational education and skills development body, is recognized in over 100 countries. It empowers learners by offering internationally certified qualifications that enhance employability and bridge the gap between education and industry needs. With the support of GEMS Education and Training, City & Guilds is expanding its footprint in Pakistan to uplift the nation’s workforce.

Courses Now Accredited and Delivered by NHU

Nazeer Hussain University, now a fully accredited City & Guilds center, is offering internationally recognized training programs that cater to the growing demand for technical and professional skills. These programs are tailored to empower youth and improve employment prospects, both locally and abroad. The following City & Guilds accredited courses are currently being delivered at NHU:

  • Supply Chain and Logistics Management
  • Digital Marketing Essentials
  • English Skills for Workplace Communication
  • Pharmacy Technician Program
  • 3D Design and Visualization (3Ds MAX & V-Ray)
  • Advanced Photoshop Techniques
  • Coding and Programming Basics
  • Electrical Basics
  • Advanced Financial Accounting Workshop
  • Project Management Foundations

Each course is meticulously designed to meet global industry standards and is supported by hands-on training, expert faculty, and modern facilities.

A Transformational Opportunity for Pakistan’s Youth

This collaboration between NHU, City & Guilds, and GEMS Education and Training provides a golden opportunity for Pakistani youth to gain globally relevant skills and certifications without needing to go abroad. These programs open doors to careers in high-demand sectors such as digital marketing, healthcare, IT, design, engineering, and project management.

GEMS remains committed to elevating educational quality across Pakistan, helping institutions like NHU become centers of excellence in vocational and professional training. The accreditation ceremony stands as a testament to the collective efforts to transform the nation’s education system and equip its youth with the tools they need for a brighter future.
